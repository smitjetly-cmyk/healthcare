Patient Testing Process

  1. Patient Registration – sign up online or at the front desk.
  2. Sample Collection – blood, urine or other samples collected by trained staff.
  3. Laboratory Testing – samples analysed using automated equipment.
  4. Report Generation – results verified by a pathologist.
  5. Doctor Consultation – discuss the report with your physician.

Common Test Abbreviations

CBC
Complete Blood Count – measures red cells, white cells and platelets.
HbA1c
Glycated Haemoglobin – average blood sugar level over 2–3 months.
LFT
Liver Function Test – checks liver enzyme and protein levels.
KFT
Kidney Function Test – assesses creatinine and urea levels.
ECG
Electrocardiogram – records the electrical activity of the heart.

Note: fasting may be required before certain tests such as HbA1c and Lipid Profile.
