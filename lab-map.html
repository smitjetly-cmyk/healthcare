Urine Test Strip Parameters

Glucose
Detects abnormal sugar levels in urine.
Protein
Screens for excess protein in urine.
Blood
Detects the presence of blood in urine.
Ketone
Indicates the presence of ketone bodies.
pH
Measures urine acidity or alkalinity.
