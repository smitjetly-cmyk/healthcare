Urine Test Strip Details

Glucose Pad

The glucose pad detects the presence of glucose or sugar in urine.

Abnormal glucose levels in urine may indicate increased blood sugar levels.

Purpose: Detection of glucose in urine.

Protein Pad

The protein pad detects excess protein present in urine.

It can be useful for screening possible kidney-related problems.

Purpose: Detection of protein in urine.

Blood Pad

The blood pad detects the presence of blood in urine.

The presence of blood in urine is known as haematuria.

Purpose: Detection of blood in urine.

Ketone Pad

The ketone pad detects ketone bodies present in urine.

Ketones may be produced when the body uses fat as an energy source.

Purpose: Detection of ketones in urine.

pH Pad

The pH pad measures the acidity or alkalinity of urine.

It provides an approximate pH value of the urine sample.

Purpose: Measurement of urine pH.
